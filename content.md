## 2. Home

**Page job:** recognition first, then the approach, then evidence through the demo.
**Key message:** KalpRiti designs the story, the interaction and the next step as one campaign experience.
**Proof on the page:** the live demo, and the Keus POC preview, labelled.

### Section 1: Hero

**Headline:** Your product does more than your customers understand.

**Subhead:** KalpRiti is a founder-led creative studio. We build campaign experiences where the story, the interaction and the next step are designed together, so people see why your product matters to them.

**Buttons:** Discuss a Campaign · Explore the Interactive Experience

*Alternate headline if you want something warmer:* "People are looking at your product. Are they understanding it?"

### Section 2: The gap

**Heading:** The gap shows up in ordinary places.

- People click your ad, then leave without knowing what the product does.
- Your sales team starts every call with the basics.
- A premium product gets compared on price, because its value isn't visible.
- One message tries to speak to buyers who each care about something different.

**Closing line:** Sometimes the product isn't the problem. The gap between its value and what customers understand is.

### Section 3: Why it keeps happening

**Heading:** Most campaigns say the same thing to everyone.

A film builds interest. An ad lists features. A landing page sends everyone to the same overview. Then the customer is left to work out what it means for their own life.

That's three pieces that never quite became one.

### Section 4: The approach

**Heading:** One campaign experience, not three separate pieces.

- **The story** decides what a customer should understand first.
- **The interaction** lets them explore what matters to them.
- **The next step** is offered based on what they showed they care about.

The interactive part isn't added after the creative is finished. The story decides what a person explores. What they explore decides what they learn, and what they're offered next.

## 3. What We Do

**Page job:** make each offer clear enough to choose from, without prices or timelines.

### Header

**Three ways to work with us.**
Start with the thinking, build the experience, or keep improving it after launch. We talk through scope and investment on a call, once we understand what you're trying to do.

### Offer 1: Campaign Strategy Sprint

**For teams who know there's a communication gap and want a clear plan before anything is built.**

**What you get**
- Alignment on the objective and the audience
- A review of how the product is communicated today
- Insight and messaging direction
- A campaign narrative
- Creative direction
- A blueprint for the interactive experience

**Not included:** building the experience, production, media buying, ongoing execution. We scope those separately if you want them.

### Offer 2: Interactive Campaign Experience

**For teams with a launch, campaign or acquisition goal who want it built, not just planned.**

**What you get**
- Strategy and narrative
- A campaign concept and creative direction
- Concepts for how people arrive (the ad, the film, the link)
- One core interactive experience
- Agreed paths through it, with next steps matched to each
- An analytics plan, with setup where we have access
- Testing and launch support

**Not included:** media spend or media buying, extensive production, complex CRM or backend integrations, hosting and software costs, unlimited revisions, and additional audiences or objectives beyond what we agree.

**A note on what you already have:** a product film, campaign or landing page can stay as the entry point if it works. We'd only suggest redoing it if it's fundamentally unclear.

### Offer 3: Campaign Growth Partner

**For teams who've launched and want to keep improving what they've built.** It usually follows an initial campaign or experience.

**What you get**
- Ongoing campaign planning
- A defined round of improvements to the campaign or experience
- Narrative and creative development
- Refinements to the paths, interactions and next steps
- A review of how it's performing, based on the data available

**Not included:** media buying, unlimited creative volume, extensive production, guaranteed performance.

### What we don't promise

We don't promise results. We can't honestly tell you what a campaign will do for your sales before it has been tested. We'll agree what to measure up front, and we'll be straight with you about what the numbers show.

### Who this is for

- You sell a valuable product that's hard to explain: technical, unfamiliar, premium, or with several different uses.
- Customers need time to understand, compare or involve others before they buy.
- You have a real launch, campaign or acquisition goal.
- A decision-maker or champion is involved.
- You're ready to invest in strategy and a custom experience, and to share what you know about your product.

### Who it isn't for

- You need routine social posts, ad creatives or low-cost design.
- You see creativity as decoration, or want interactivity added without a reason.
- You need guaranteed conversions or revenue.
- You expect strategy, creative and a custom build on a very small budget, or in a few days.
- You can't involve the people who make the decision.
- Your product is a quick, low-consideration purchase, where customers decide in seconds.

### How a project moves **[CONFIRM: derived from the offers, adjust to your real process]**

1. **Understand.** We learn the product, the buyer and where understanding breaks down.
2. **Shape the story.** We decide what customers should understand first, and what they should discover.
3. **Build the experience.** The campaign and the interaction are designed and made together.
4. **Learn.** We look at what the data shows and decide what to change.

## 8. Brief for development

**No code here.** This is written for a developer or a no-code builder to work from.

### Look and feel
- Clean, editorial, cinematic, premium but approachable.
- Warm cream background, vermilion as the accent, deep burgundy for depth.
- Generous whitespace, strong typography, restrained layouts.
- Liquid-glass effects only in selected places (for example the demo panel and the nav), not everywhere.
- Motion with a purpose: transitions that guide attention and give feedback, not decoration. Respect reduced-motion settings.
- Short paragraphs. Nothing long or dense.

### Pages to build
Home, What We Do, Work, About, Contact, a thank-you state for the form, a privacy note and a 404.

### The demo (most important piece)
- Four choices on Home. Selecting one changes four things: the **order and emphasis** of the explanation, the **supporting text**, the **link under it**, and the **button**.
- The adaptation must visibly go beyond swapping a single block: the other three choices stay available, and the visitor can switch without a page reload.
- It responds **only to the explicit choice**. No hidden behavioural tracking is used to decide what appears.
- No email or sign-up needed. The demo is never gated.
- The explainer ("What just happened") appears after the first choice.
- Button logic: choices 1, 3 and 4 show **Discuss a Campaign**. Choice 2 shows **Discuss a Communication Challenge**. All lead to the same Contact page, and the form can note which one the visitor came from.

### Contact form
- Six fields as listed, with the stated options. Required: name, email, company or website, what they're trying to communicate, role.
- Sends an automatic confirmation email and notifies you.
- Shows the thank-you state with the "what happens next" steps.
- Must keep the 24-hour promise honest: set an internal reminder for every submission.

### Work page
- The POC tag must be visible at all times, near the title and in any embed's caption.
- Embed the live Keus experience if it is ready and stable, otherwise use a recorded walkthrough.
- Leave room to add more experiments later, one at a time, without redesigning the page.

### Content and tone rules for anyone editing later
- Never publish prices, timelines, performance claims, client logos, or a team that doesn't exist.
- Do not mention Dyson anywhere.
- Do not mention an email resource until it exists.
- Do not add an origin or meaning for the name KalpRiti.
- Use "creative studio," not "agency," as the main descriptor.

### Updates after launch
Structure the Work page so one substantial addition every 4-6 weeks is easy. Do not build a blog or news section.

### Launch checklist
1. Keus build confirmed stable and labelled as a POC.
2. Demo tested on mobile and desktop.
3. Form submissions reach you, and confirmation emails send.
4. Privacy note matches what the form and analytics actually collect.
5. All **[CONFIRM]** items resolved.
6. SEO titles and descriptions added after you approve the narrative.
